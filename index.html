How to use

1. Selection order matters

Boards play in the order you select them. Click each layer, group, or board one by one in the desired playback order.

frame_1 → frame_2 → frame_3

2. Per-frame delay override

Append :N or :Nms to a layer name to override the global delay for that single frame.

  • intro:500 → 500 ms
  • pause:1000ms → 1000 ms
  • quick → uses the global delay

Values under 20 ms fall back to the global delay.

3. Transparent background

Pick White or Black in the Background field. Any pixel of that color in your design becomes fully transparent in the output GIF.

Choose a color that doesn't appear inside your artwork — every matching pixel will be knocked out.

4. Quality & size

Under Advanced, drop the color count (e.g. 64) for lighter files. Use Scale to export at 0.5×, 1× or 2× the source resolution.

5. Saving the GIF
